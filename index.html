what this does

MediaPipe's face landmarker finds your eyes and irises 30-60 times per second. When your irises drift away from the eye center (you looking at notes, a second screen, the timeline of the meeting you're in), the demo shifts the eye region back toward the lens, so on the call you look like you're paying attention.

This is the browser version of OpenBroadcast, which does the same job with trained models, a calibration wizard and better warping. This one trades quality for the fact that you just clicked a link and it worked.

Frames are processed on your device and never uploaded anywhere. View source if you don't believe me.

how to use it

  1. Allow camera access when the browser asks.
  2. Look at your camera. The dots on your eyes should sit near the center.
  3. Now look slightly down or to the side, like you're reading notes. The dots drift with you.
  4. Hit correction: on. Your eyes get pulled back toward the camera while the rest of your face stays honest.

Works best with decent lighting. If the dots don't show up, move back a little so your whole face is in frame.
